## Audio and Image Assets

Source files live in `assets/`; `scripts/prepare_assets.py` produces the optimized copies in `src/assets/` that are
embedded (as data URIs) in the single-file build.

- **Scream vocal (`assets/audio/scream.mp3`)** and **menu ambience (`assets/audio/menu_ambience.mp3`)**:
  generated by the project author with an active paid commercial subscription to ElevenLabs,
  licensed and authorized for commercial and non-commercial distribution within this project.
- **Ghost scream faces (`assets/screams/*`)**: images generated by the project author for this project.
